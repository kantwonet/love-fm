## 13 October 2026 — A connected music home, with an identity of its own

**Public-showcase update; no new application-acceptance credit.**

Love’s public identity uses the approved pink-and-purple ribbon heart over a vinyl record. The portal brings together the full product vision: listening history, automatic/background scrobbling, imports, connected libraries, playlist tools, Heartbeat, Discord, the browser companion, Social and Pulse.

The public presentation distinguishes intended launch features, Preferred features, later work and ideas. Concept panels use fictional music and people; real app UI captures are deferred until suitable builds and demonstration data are ready. No public download or signup is offered here.
