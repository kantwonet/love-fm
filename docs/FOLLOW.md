# Follow Love.fm

[Open the portal](https://kantwonet.github.io/love-fm/) · [Development updates](UPDATES.md) · [Milestones](PROGRESS.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

Love is in development. Follow meaningful accepted milestones, product explanations and future availability announcements—not every internal test run.

## Choose how to keep up

**Notifications:** open [the GitHub repository](https://github.com/kantwonet/love-fm) and use **Watch** to select notification categories. When Discussions is enabled, choose **Custom → Discussions** for those updates. Delivery also depends on your GitHub notification preferences.

**A single announcement thread:** the dedicated development-updates discussion is not open yet. Its direct link will be added here once it has been created and verified. You can then subscribe to that discussion rather than all repository activity.

**Bookmark and support:** use **Star** on the repository. Starring is not the same as subscribing to announcements.

**Read without subscribing:** [Development updates](UPDATES.md) gives concise, dated notes. [Milestones](PROGRESS.md) explains accepted scope and remaining boundaries. These are public summaries, not a live view of private development.

There is no email form, public app signup or public installer on this showcase. No release date is announced here. Provider access and product acceptance remain separate requirements.

## Sharing and privacy

Share the public portal or repository. Do not post credentials, private listening records, local file paths or raw diagnostic logs in public discussions or issues.
