# Follow Love.fm

[Open development updates](https://github.com/kantwonet/love-fm/discussions/1) · [Visit the portal](https://kantwonet.github.io/love-fm/) · [Update archive](UPDATES.md) · [Milestones](PROGRESS.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

Love is in development. Follow meaningful accepted milestones, product explanations and future availability announcements—not every internal test run.

## Follow the announcement thread

Open **[Love.fm — Development updates](https://github.com/kantwonet/love-fm/discussions/1)** and choose **Subscribe** in its Notifications section. Sign in to GitHub if prompted. This follows activity in that discussion; simply opening the link does not subscribe you. If the button says **Unsubscribe**, you are already subscribed.

For broader discussion notifications, open [the repository](https://github.com/kantwonet/love-fm) and choose **Watch → Custom → Discussions**. Delivery also depends on your GitHub notification preferences.

## Other ways to keep up

**Bookmark and support:** use **Star** on the repository. Starring is not the same as subscribing to announcements.

**Read without subscribing:** the [announcement thread](https://github.com/kantwonet/love-fm/discussions/1) and [update archive](UPDATES.md) provide public development notes. [Milestones](PROGRESS.md) explains accepted scope and remaining boundaries. These are public summaries, not a live view of private development.

There is no email form, public app signup or public installer on this showcase. No release date is announced here. Provider access and product acceptance remain separate requirements.

## Sharing and privacy

Share the public portal or announcement thread. Do not post credentials, private listening records, local file paths or raw diagnostic logs in public discussions or issues.
