# Love.fm — Roadmap

[← Overview](../README.md) · [Full feature guide](FEATURES.md) · [Accepted milestones](PROGRESS.md)

**Product Blueprint 2.8 · Public summary dated 13 October 2026.** A delivery map, not a percent-complete chart. Later program areas may already contain code; acceptance must be reconciled separately.

## First-release direction

Windows 11 x64; core Library and local music; validated playback and queue behavior; listening history, imports and scrobbling; Search and Heartbeat; applicable lyrics/waveform availability; discovery; privacy and guest access; core Social and Pulse chat/media; desktop, browser and Discord integration; Copy, one-way Sync and one-time Merge on approved routes; recovery and support. All included routes need acceptance.

## Delivery structure

### A / FOUNDATION — Protect what makes it yours
Ownership, execution, startup and coordinated recovery. Current accepted checkpoints cover bounded operations; the full recovery program remains open.

### B / ONE MUSIC — Unify without flattening
Identity, provenance, library membership, playlist versions and the listening ledger. One song identity must not erase different recordings or real replays.

### C / PLAYBACK & PROVIDERS — Connect the supported routes
Local parity, source/device controls, queues, restart behavior and permitted provider capabilities. Every integration earns its own validation.

### D / MUSIC EXPERIENCES — Bring the everyday app together
Library, Search, Heartbeat, History, lyrics, Discover and the one-time Copy/Sync/Merge experiences, with explicit disposition of Preferred features.

### E / ACCOUNTS & SHARED USE — Make room for your people
Account isolation, optional cloud scope, privacy, messages, Pulse, support and responsible online operation.

### F / FINISH & LAUNCH — Make the whole product ready
Visual finish after stability, onboarding, accessibility, performance, signed distribution, update/recovery and the public capability record.

## Preferred: explicitly include or defer

Music World, selected Love cloud synchronization, extra graphs, adaptive tips, optional personal usage metrics, validated file-tag writes, crossfade and taskbar artwork. They are not silently part of the guaranteed launch scope.

## After 1.0

**Two-way Mirror:** Bidirectional collection updates with explicit baseline, conflict and loop-prevention rules.

**Live Merge:** Keep merged outputs updated from inputs, with removal choices, exclusions and retained manual additions.

**Cloud-run transfers:** Run separately approved jobs without the assigned desktop being awake.

**Listen Along:** A distinct passive-follow listening mode—not a different name for the launch-target Pulse room.

**Persistent collaborative playlists:** Shared long-lived playlist editing, separate from Pulse queue contributions.

**More platforms:** Mac, Linux and mobile expansion through separate scope and acceptance work.

**More real-time sharing:** Camera and screen sharing are deferred. Voice remains an idea requiring a later decision, not a guaranteed feature.

## Open release dependencies

Provider access and data lifecycle; per-operation capability acceptance; lyrics/enrichment rights; timestamp/import verification; Pulse timing and capacity; cloud identity/hosting; privacy and moderation policy; remaining foundation/recovery gates; measured performance/accessibility; signed distribution and support; operating capacity; Preferred-feature disposition; detailed visual validation; and future synchronization semantics. These summarize DEP-001–015—not new promises or claims that every dependency is unresolved in today’s private checkout.

No release date, overall completion percentage or provider partnership is invented. A plan can be ambitious while its status stays precise.
